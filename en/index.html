01 — THE TIER 2 POSITION

In the middle, to protect both ends.

Tier 1 owns and controls. Tier 3 operates and carries market risk. Tier 2 sits between them, holding the IP so that an incident at a farm or a plant can never reach the group's core assets.

  1. Tier 1

    PAA Empire Holdings

    Owns 100% of PAA IP Holdings, sets strategy and approves the IP portfolio.

    Ownership & control ↓

  2. Tier 2

    PAA IP Holdings

    Registers, protects, holds and licenses intellectual property. No production, no outside dealings.

    Exclusive licences ↓

  3. Tier 3

    LAOS Agro Processing · KEOPHILA · PAA Agri Development

    Use the IP under licence agreements, within the agreed scope, term and standards.

02

02 — THREE IRONCLAD PRINCIPLES

  1. I

    Hold all core intellectual property.

    Every formula, process, piece of software and trademark created in the ecosystem is registered and held here — not scattered across the operating companies.

  2. II

    No debt. No collateral. No outside dealings.

    IP is never used as security, never sold and never licensed outside the group.

  3. III

    Internal licences only, every contract on record.

    Each Tier 3 company uses IP through a licence with a clear scope, term and conditions, reviewed on a regular cycle.

04 — INTERNAL LICENSING

Four steps, one file per licence.

  1. 1

    Licence request

    A Tier 3 company submits a request: which asset, for what purpose, where and for how long.

  2. 2

    Review & approval

    Legal reviews scope, risk and confidentiality conditions; Tier 1 approves material assets.

  3. 3

    Licence agreement

    An internal licence is signed: scope, term, confidentiality duties and quality standards.

  4. 4

    Monitoring & periodic reconciliation

    Use is tracked, compliance reviewed and reconciled each period; renewed or adjusted as needed.
